## Keep rules and tasks synchronized

Keep stable operating rules and durable candidate calibration in current rule/profile references. Keep named people, applications, outcomes, company watches and commitments in live owning records. A continuation note points to those records rather than becoming an authoritative snapshot.

Latest explicit candidate corrections govern rules. Current owning-workflow rules override conflicting legacy task instructions. Current records govern recorded state; original evidence establishes events. Update equivalent tasks in place, preserving identity, integrations, permissions and schedule unless a change is approved. If editing is unavailable, identify the existing task and provide replacement instructions rather than creating a duplicate.

## Tracker field mapping

Map by actual header rather than assumed position. These defaults describe Tracker template 0.3 and do not authorize changing another tracker’s schema.

| Meaning | Template 0.3 default | Your actual field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General relationship links | Roles, Person IDs, S | [Header/location] |
| Potential or verified person names under current warm-path rules | Roles, Warm Path / Contact, X | [Header/location] |
| Assistance evidence and history | Roles, Notes, W; linked Activities | [Header/location] |
| Hiring milestone | Roles, Hiring stage, G | [Header/location and valid values] |
| Date the hiring milestone changed | Roles, Stage change date, I | [Header/location] |
| Process outcome | Roles, Process outcome, H | [Header/location and valid values] |

Warm Path / Contact contains actual names only, separated by semicolons, or is blank. Potential paths can be recorded before application. Applied/later and terminal records require reliable evidence of concrete opportunity-specific help. Retain legitimate helpers after rejection or closure. Keep general Person IDs even when a person does not qualify as a warm path. A warm-path-only correction never changes hiring stage or its date. Follow the full Tracker rules for qualification and preserve history.

## Task verification record

For each configured task, record its actual ID, current saved instructions and resolved source references, schedule/timezone, scope, read/write authorization, result destination and run-log location. Track these results separately: instructions saved, installed configuration read back, manual trial completed, scheduled run observed. Mark untested behavior Untested. A prompt save or successful connector read does not prove a task ran or delivered results.

## Verify before relying on the map

Have the assistant open the referenced files and confirm what it can read. Confirm they are your working copies. Use attachments, pasted text and exact manual tracker entries when tools are unavailable. Keep one authoritative artifact per reference; update existing files rather than creating unnecessary duplicates. Keep credentials out of this document.
