## Verify before relying on the map

Open each referenced artifact, confirm it belongs to your workspace, and mark missing materials as Missing. Have the assistant state what it can access. Use a manual copy-and-paste path where needed. Read current records before writes, deduplicate before adding, and verify saved changes. Keep credentials out of this document.
