# Job Hunting OS Guide - Start Here

A reusable job-search kit for working with an AI assistant. Begin with the setup steps below, then use the topic prompts and optional recurring tasks as needed. You do not need the original creator’s conversations or handoffs.

**Supporting files:** [Workspace Map](Workspace-Map.md) · [Blank Tracker](Job-Hunting-OS-Tracker.xlsx)

**Navigate:** [Setup](#1-create-a-folder-for-your-job-search) · [Opening prompt](#6-start-the-work-in-the-chat-you-opened) · [Working prompts](#9-working-conversation-prompts) · [Recurring tasks](#10-set-up-recurring-tasks) · [Manual alternatives](#12-manual-use-missing-access-and-new-chats)

Version 0.1. Initial repository package. Scheduled tasks are instructions to configure and test in your own assistant, not installed services. Independent-candidate testing is still pending.


The Job Hunting OS is a set of guides and a spreadsheet you use with an AI assistant to organize your job search. OS means operating system here, a way of working, not software you need to install. You can use the AI assistant you prefer, such as ChatGPT or Claude.

This guide helps you set up your own files and start your first conversation. Begin with your resume and LinkedIn profile. Then work on finding jobs, contacting people, preparing for interviews, and deciding what to do next. You do not need to understand the whole system before starting.

## 1 Create a folder for your job search

In Google Drive, choose New, then New folder. Name the folder Job Hunting OS. This is where you will keep your own job-search documents and spreadsheet. If your AI assistant has permission and the tools to create a folder, you can ask it to do this for you.

You can also use another storage service. The important thing is to have one place where you can find your files again.

## 2 Get your two supporting files

Download [Job-Hunting-OS-Tracker.xlsx](Job-Hunting-OS-Tracker.xlsx). On GitHub, open the file and use the download button; the file preview is not your editable workbook.

For Google Sheets, upload the downloaded workbook to your own Drive folder, open it in Google Sheets, and choose File → Save as Google Sheets if it opens in Office editing mode. Keep the converted workbook in your folder and name it Job Hunting OS Tracker. You can also use the downloaded workbook in Excel. Check that the eight tabs, dropdowns and Dashboard formulas work in your chosen application before relying on it.

Open [Workspace-Map.md](Workspace-Map.md). Copy its text into a document in your own folder and name it Job Hunting OS Workspace Map, or download the Markdown file and edit your private copy. Markdown is plain text with formatting; you do not need Git or a code editor to use the map. Replace its placeholders with your own information.

This README is the complete guide. You can read it here or save a private copy. The repository contains only this guide and those two blank supporting files. Keep your filled map, career materials and live tracker in your own private workspace, not in a public repository.

## 3 Add the resume and profile you already have

Put your newest resume in your folder, even if it needs updating. Add a document containing your LinkedIn headline, About section and work history, or a current profile export if you have one. If you do not have a resume or LinkedIn profile yet, you can create them with the assistant.

Optional: add notes about projects, responsibilities, achievements, results or feedback that could help explain your experience. These are what the system calls Career Evidence. You do not need to gather everything before you begin.

## 4 Open a chat with your AI assistant

Open one new conversation in your chosen AI assistant and name it Job Hunting OS Setup and Weekly Review. Keep this chat open while you complete Step 5. In Step 6, you will paste the opening message that starts the work. You can keep using this same chat for setup and later weekly reviews.

Give the assistant the materials it needs by attaching files, pasting relevant text, or using a file connection you have authorized. A Drive link alone may not give it access. Ask it to confirm what it can read. If it cannot open a file, attach it or paste the relevant text instead.

## 5 Fill in the list of your files

Open your copy of Job Hunting OS Workspace Map. Replace the square-bracket placeholders with your name, chosen AI assistant, timezone, folder location and the links to your own files. In Google Drive, open a file and use Share, then Copy link to get its link. Copying a link does not require making the file public.

The map is simply a directory for you and your assistant. For example, Current Resume means the resume you added in Step 3. Tracker Roles means the Roles sheet inside your copied tracker, not another document. Career Evidence means your optional achievement notes. Interview Learning will mean notes you create after interviews; you can leave that reference marked Missing for now.

Fill in what you have and mark anything else Missing or Not set up. You do not need to create scheduled tasks or connect email to begin. Share the completed map with your assistant along with your resume and LinkedIn text.

## 6 Start the work in the chat you opened

Paste the message below into the chat you opened in Step 4, with the files you supplied in Step 5. This is the one starting prompt for the toolkit. You do not need another guide or a second setup conversation.

Work with the assistant on your resume and LinkedIn materials first. If they are ready, move on quickly. If they need work, the assistant helps recover and update your experience. Then continue in the same chat to choose your search direction and begin using the tracker in Step 7.

The assistant should guide each next step rather than send you back through another set of setup instructions. You can pause and return to the same chat at any time.

### Copy this message into your chat



```text
Help me begin my job search using the Job Hunting OS. I am providing my list of files, called the Workspace Map, and the resume and LinkedIn information I currently have. Tell me which materials you can actually read and what is missing. Do not assume that a link gives you access.
First explain briefly how you can help, then ask whether I am ready to begin. Start by asking whether my resume and LinkedIn profile are up to date and whether I am satisfied with them. If they are ready, check that the facts agree and help me move on. If they need updating or do not exist, help me build them, asking one useful question at a time. Include my most recent job and use only facts I can support. Distinguish what I personally did from work I led. Show me proposed changes and report a file as updated only after the change is saved and checked.
Ask about my profession, what I want next, my timing, location and work-arrangement needs, employment types, hard constraints, interests and weekly capacity. Keep preferences separate from hard constraints and leave unknowns explicit. Once my career materials are usable, help me save a short profile, verified accomplishment notes and a search strategy in my own folder, and add their locations to my Workspace Map. Give me ready-to-save text if you cannot create documents.
Continue in this same chat to help me populate my tracker. Read its How This Works instructions, headers and allowed values. Ask for a job I am considering or help find a few matching roles where research is available. Ask about a few relevant people and choose one to three actions with me. Use only verified facts, assign stable IDs, check duplicates and leave unsupported dates or states unknown.
Explain whether you can edit my copied tracker and what access or permission is needed. If editing is available and authorized, enter the agreed records and verify them. Otherwise give me exact entries grouped by tab and field to paste, then ask me to share the saved rows for checking. Do not imply I must design the entries myself. Preserve the tracker’s schema and history. Drafts are not sent messages and job alerts are not applications.
Keep this manageable and ask one useful question at a time. Draft messages and applications for review; do not send or submit without explicit authorization. Finish setup with usable career materials, a saved search direction, a small set of tracker records and next actions, or clearly identified blockers. Save a short continuation note with my file references, current rules, decisions and pending work. Then help me choose the next useful action. Refer to Section 9 of this guide for topic-chat prompts or Section 10 for optional recurring tasks. Do not restart onboarding.
```

## 7 Build your first tracker records with the assistant

Stay in the same chat and provide your copied Job Hunting OS Tracker. The assistant should now help choose and record a few jobs, contacts and next actions. You do not have to fill the spreadsheet before asking for help.

If the assistant can edit and you authorize it, it enters the agreed records and checks them. If it cannot edit, it gives you exact entries to paste into the named tabs, then checks the saved rows you share. You supply the facts and make decisions; the assistant helps organize and enter them.

The tabs along the bottom are separate sheets within the same spreadsheet. Here is what each one is for.

| Tab | What to put here |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | A summary calculated from the records you enter, including recent activity and open tasks. |
| Roles | Jobs you are considering or have applied for, with their details and progress. |
| People | People you know or want to contact, how you know them, and relevant conversations. |
| Target Companies | Employers you are interested in, even if you have not found a specific job there. |
| Networking Events | Events you are considering, registered for, or have attended. |
| Activities & Follow-ups | What you plan to do, when it is due, and what you actually completed. |
| Interviews | Interview dates and references to your preparation and notes afterward. |
| How This Works | Instructions for entering records and understanding the spreadsheet’s labels. |



Begin with one to three roles, a few relevant contacts and one to three agreed actions, or fewer if that is all you have. The assistant can assess a posting you supply or research roles when access is available. It must not invent contacts or hiring progress. Empty tabs are fine.

Have the assistant read How This Works before preparing entries, or supply its text if it cannot open the sheet. It explains the available choices and unique IDs, labels such as R-0001 that connect records across tabs. The Dashboard updates from saved records, whether you or an authorized assistant entered them. It does not search your inbox or track actions automatically.

If using Google Sheets, use File, then Settings to set your timezone. In Excel, enter dates using your local date convention. Enter dates consistently. The Dashboard initially shows the last seven days; you can change its start and end dates. Each data sheet supports 500 records. If you reach that limit, ask for help extending the tables and formulas before adding more.

## 8 Choose your next action

Stay in the same setup chat until you have usable career materials, a saved search direction, a few tracker records and one to three next actions, or clear blockers. You do not need every tab filled or scheduled tasks enabled.

The rest of this guide is a menu for ongoing use, not another onboarding sequence. Use Section 9 when a dedicated topic chat would help. Use Section 10 when you want scheduled help. Use Section 12 if tools are unavailable. You can keep using your setup chat for weekly reviews.



## 9 Working-conversation prompts

Create these chats as you need them. There is no required number of chats. In a new chat, paste its prompt and supply your map plus the current files it needs. If your assistant cannot access shared project files, attach or paste them. A brief context packet can orient it, but current records must be read before edits.

Save decisions and useful outputs in your own documents or tracker, rather than relying on a chat as the only record. Add each chat’s current rules and durable file references to the Workflow section of your map. When a rule changes, update the durable rule record and affected recurring task instructions.



### Chat: Career Materials

Use when: reviewing or tailoring your resume, LinkedIn text, professional introduction or career stories.

Provide: Current Resume, Current LinkedIn, Career Evidence, Candidate Profile and any target posting.

```text
Own my career facts and materials. Resolve my files through my Workspace Map and state what you can access. Read the current approved baseline and my latest corrections before using old versions.
Ask whether I need a quick review, a targeted update or a rebuild. Recover missing recent work: mandate, scope, decisions, collaborators, ownership, outcomes and lessons. Separate personal implementation, technical leadership, organizational leadership and team delivery. Do not invent metrics or turn an observed improvement into a proven causal claim.
Keep a concise evidence record for reusable accomplishments, with sources, qualifiers and confidentiality limits. Adapt emphasis to my profession and target audience while keeping titles, dates and claims consistent. Tailored resumes derive from the approved baseline and identify the target role.
Show proposed changes and unresolved factual questions. Save authorized edits in my files and verify them before saying updated. An export or proposed LinkedIn text does not prove the live profile changed. Draft public changes for review.
Stop when materials are accurate, readable and usable. Finish with what changed, what needs my confirmation and the smallest useful next step.
```



### Chat: Roles and Applications

Use when: assessing a posting, reviewing the queue, tailoring an application or recording submission.

Provide: Search Strategy, Career Evidence, Tracker Roles, related People and Activities, and the posting.

```text
Manage my opportunity pipeline. Use my Workspace Map, saved search criteria and current tracker contract. Confirm access and inspect current headers, dropdowns and affected rows before writes. Preserve an existing tracker’s schema.
Assess actual responsibilities, constraints, interests and evidence match. Separate hard eligibility rules, narrative fit, priority and action order. Missing information is a question to verify, not automatically a concrete concern. Explain any P2 concern under my configured definitions.
Prefer employer or official ATS evidence for posting facts; preserve the real discovery source separately. State when a source cannot be checked. An inaccessible page is not proof a vacancy closed.
Deduplicate by employer, requisition ID or canonical URL and role scope; keep distinct requisitions separate. Keep anonymous recruiter searches separate until identity is verified. Preserve confidentiality.
Keep posting availability, fit/pursuit, hiring milestone and process outcome separate. Preserve application evidence and actual submission date after later outcomes. Posting removal does not end an active candidacy. Change the stage date only when the hiring stage changes. Do not infer application, rejection or referral from a draft, alert or silence.
Rank only the pre-application queue allowed by my tracker. Clear rank after application without hiding active interview and follow-up needs. Put next actions in the mapped action records.
For promising roles, check verified contacts and hiring-team paths; distinguish confirmed ownership from inferred relevance. Coordinate referrals and recruiter outreach without indefinitely delaying time-sensitive applications.
Prepare tailored materials from approved career claims. Draft, but do not submit applications or messages without explicit authorization. Verify authorized tracker edits. Report qualifying roles, evidence, gaps and at most three actions. Say when none qualifies.
```



### Chat: People and Networking

Use when: reconnecting, planning outreach, responding to a contact, or recording help and commitments.

Provide: People, relevant Roles and Activities, plus actual conversation context.

```text
Manage my professional relationships using my Workspace Map and current People and Activities records. Verify identities with email, profile URL and context; name alone is provisional. Check current affiliation before calling someone an insider.
Read recent conversations and sent messages where authorized before recommending another contact. Distinguish relationship strength from priority and activity. An invitation received or accepted is not a conversation, a sent message or a referral.
Recommend a small group appropriate to my capacity. For each person, explain why contact is useful now and use the established channel, relationship context and one proportionate ask. Never invent familiarity, shared history or willingness to help. Keep my voice.
Draft outreach and replies for review. Send only when explicitly authorized. Record sent outreach only with evidence or my confirmation. Record help offered, accepted and completed separately. A referral requires a verified role-specific submission; an introduction requires an actual introduction.
Deduplicate contacts and activities. Link relevant person and role IDs. Record commitments and due dates in the mapped action system, reconcile completed actions, and preserve relationship history after a role ends. Do not change hiring stage because outreach occurred.
Save authorized updates only after checking current rows; verify them. End with one to three useful actions, optionally a draft for the first. Missing records mean unknown, not that no relationship exists.
```



### Chat: Target Companies

Use when: choosing employers to watch or investigating a company.

Provide: Search Strategy, Target Companies, Roles and related People.

```text
Manage my employer shortlist using my Workspace Map and current records. Assess business, role families, constraints, interests, evidence match, concerns and credible relationship paths. Explain a company thesis instead of relying on brand appeal.
Separate watch membership from posting status, candidate hiring stage/outcome and fit/pursuit. Removing a company from one watch does not exclude it from every search. Confirm the scope of unclear removal instructions and preserve useful historical records.
Verify actual employers behind investor portfolios and recruiter routes. Keep confidential searches restricted and do not approach people around them without explicit instruction.
Check official openings, apply the current Roles rules and deduplicate against existing opportunities. Route qualifying roles into the same pipeline, not a competing company queue. Do not force a match because an employer is attractive.
Reconcile stale summaries with newer supported role outcomes and completed commitments. Preserve broader relationships when a process ends. Distinguish last research date from stage dates and action due dates.
Make only authorized, relevant record changes and verify them. Do not migrate schemas or apply, message or register under company-review authority. Give a focused shortlist or meaningful changes, coverage limits and at most three actions.
```



### Chat: Networking Events

Use when: finding professional events, deciding whether to attend or following through afterward.

Provide: Search Strategy, event preferences, Events, People and Activities.

```text
Help me choose professional events around my goals, expertise, geography, availability, budget and capacity. Use my Workspace Map and current records. Ask about missing preferences when they materially affect a decision.
Verify organizer, occurrence date/time and timezone, venue or virtual format, availability, price, deadlines, agenda and intended audience. Distinguish confirmed attendees from an inferred audience. An AI or industry label is not proof of advanced content or meaningful interaction.
Recommend GO, MAYBE or SKIP with a specific reason and uncertainties. Evaluate relevant people, conversation potential, learning beyond my current expertise, logistics and value for money. A price objection on one event is not a universal cap.
Keep your recommendation separate from my decision and participation state. Registration needs confirmation; attendance needs actual participation evidence. Match recurring events by occurrence date/time, organizer and URL; update the same record through its lifecycle.
After attendance, help capture meaningful conversations, useful learning and commitments. Deduplicate actual contacts and route follow-ups to People and Activities, concrete vacancies to Roles and target ideas to Companies. Attendance alone establishes none of those.
Draft follow-ups for review. Do not buy tickets, register or send messages without explicit authorization. Verify authorized updates and keep the immediate action list small.
```



### Chat: Interview Preparation and Learning

Use when: preparing for a recruiter screen, interview or important career conversation, and debriefing afterward.

Provide: role description, interview details, correspondence, Career Evidence, prior notes and related records.

```text
Prepare me for an effective conversation, using my Workspace Map, current opportunity, latest correspondence and verified career evidence. Adapt to my profession, level, interview stage and interviewer. State inaccessible inputs and label assumptions.
Create a concise opportunity brief: business, mandate, relevant technical or professional context, interviewer perspective, likely success criteria and mutual-fit risks. Map important requirements to my actual stories, personal contribution, decisions, tradeoffs, outcomes and lessons.
Help me explain examples at roughly 30 seconds, two minutes and deeper follow-up depth. Use Answer, Evidence, Business implication as a flexible communication guide. Do not script every word or inflate causal claims.
Prepare likely questions, useful questions to ask and questions that reveal constraints, authority, resources and alignment. Offer practice one question at a time. Wait for my answer, say what worked, identify one specific improvement and let me retry. Use qualitative feedback unless I request scores. Prioritize practice when the interview is imminent.
Afterward capture facts, topics, strong and difficult moments, new information, observed signals, my fit assessment, commitments and next steps. Do not assume interviewer intentions.
Check previous correspondence before drafting follow-up. Drafted, sent and replied are separate states. Preserve learning even after rejection. Save reusable stories and Q&A in mapped documents, company-specific briefs with the opportunity, and follow-up tasks in Activities.
Update only authorized records under their owning rules; do not create competing pipeline states. Verify saved changes. Never send a follow-up without explicit authorization.
```

A LinkedIn notification does not prove a message remains unanswered. Missing email confirmation does not prove an external task is incomplete.
Use actual evidence to reconcile application submission, hiring milestones, outcomes, interviews and commitments under the current tracker rules. Label only explicit submitted/received application confirmations if label changes are authorized. Exclude saved jobs, application-started notices, verification, marketing and rejections treated as new applications. Preserve evidence labels after later rejection. Do not create a label taxonomy, archive or delete under review authority.
Deduplicate events and tasks using durable message IDs and event types where available. Save a run log in my folder, mapped as Task Run Log, with processed evidence, changes, unresolved items and checkpoint. Do not claim this log exists until saved.
Apply clearly evidenced changes only within recorded write authorization; otherwise show proposed edits. Verify writes and labels. Draft replies for review; never send, apply or change external settings without explicit instruction.

### Chat: Weekly Review

Use when: choosing what matters this week and updating your strategy.

Provide: Dashboard, Roles, People, Activities, Interviews, Events and your current capacity.

```text
Review my job search using my Workspace Map, current records and reporting period. Ask briefly how much time and energy I have this week. Do not treat incomplete records as evidence of failure.
Separate dated activity from cumulative totals and current pipeline state. Review applications, interviews, meaningful replies, help offered and completed, commitments, stalled opportunities, event outcomes and learning. Count distinct actions once using stable IDs. An offered referral is not a submitted referral.
Include active Applied and interviewing roles even though their application ranks are blank. Check due actions against evidence and completed work before repeating reminders. Treat silence as unknown, not rejection.
Discuss what recommendations and activities are teaching us. Propose adjustments to goals or preferences for my decision; do not silently turn one pass into a global exclusion.
Choose one to three actions adjusted to my capacity, each with a reason, small first step and appropriate due date. Save authorized decisions and tasks; verify changes. Preserve history and formulas. Draft external communications only.
Finish with progress worth recognizing, any uncertainty that changes decisions, and a manageable plan. Update my reusable context packet and current rule references when needed.
```



## 10 Set up recurring tasks

Start with one or two tasks that reduce work for you. Suggested cadences below are editable starting points, not required schedules. Use your local timezone.

Before scheduling, choose the task’s name, cadence/time/timezone, input files and rule references, email scope if needed, permitted writes, look-back or search horizon, output destination and notification preference. For a missing setting, write Not configured instead of guessing.

Use read-only mode first unless you have already authorized routine writes. To enable writes, name the candidate-owned files/tabs and allowed operations in your Workspace Map. Evidence labeling is a separate permission. External messages and applications require explicit authorization beyond these tasks.

A scheduler may have fewer tools than an interactive chat. Verify the scheduled environment can read current files, research sources, use email and perform the intended writes. If it only supports reminders, make a reminder to run the task manually rather than promising autonomous research.

Ask your assistant to inspect existing tasks before creating one. Edit a matching task in place and preserve its identity. If editing is unavailable, provide its complete replacement instructions and identify the task needing an update; do not make a duplicate replacement.

For each task, copy the Common task instructions below followed by its individual Task block. Fill the configuration with your own references. Include those references in the actual saved task; do not assume it can see another chat.

Run a supported test, or a read-only manual trial if scheduled tests are unavailable. Verify coverage, record matching, output delivery and allowed writes. Record task ID/reference, exact saved instructions, cadence, timezone, enabled state and test result in your map or Task Run Log. Mark untested items untested. Confirm that a task is installed only after successful configuration readback.

You are setting up your own tasks here. This guide does not install, enable or alter anyone’s scheduled tasks.

```text

Profile and Search Strategy: [my own references]
Current workflow rules: [durable reference or included rules]
Tracker and authorized tabs: [my own tracker reference and tab names]
Mode: [read-only OR precise authorized write operations]
Email account/scope and evidence label: [my choices OR not used]
Cadence, time and timezone: [my choices]
Window/horizon and output limit: [my choices]
Report destination and notification preference: [supported destination]
Task Run Log: [my own persistent file OR manual log]
Read affected records immediately before writes. Use stable IDs and deduplicate roles, people, events and real activities. Latest candidate corrections govern rules; current owning-workflow rules override stale task instructions. Current records govern recorded state, and original evidence establishes events. Reconcile conflicts rather than overwriting newer data.
Preserve schema, dropdowns, formulas, history and unrelated fields. Separate posting availability, hiring milestone, process outcome, fit decision and watch membership. Preserve application evidence after closure. Change stage dates only when the corresponding hiring stage changes. Record due actions in the mapped activity system. Draft, sent, replied, offered and completed are different states.
Do not send messages, submit applications, register for events, spend money, publish changes, delete or archive under these task instructions. Limit routine writes to the explicit configured scope and verify results. Otherwise report proposed edits.
Use a run log with task/run identifier, as-of time/timezone, sources checked and blocked, processed message/event IDs where applicable, proposed/applied/failed changes and unresolved items. Resume from the last successful checkpoint with configured overlap. Advance checkpoints only for coverage actually completed; retain failed or ambiguous events for retry. Without persistent logging, state that rerun deduplication is limited and remain read-only unless another verified mechanism is configured.
Coordinate with other tasks by matching the same records and rereading affected rows. If conflict-safe writes cannot be assured, propose updates instead of running overlapping writers. Do not claim locking or automatic synchronization without an implemented mechanism.
Report material findings, verified versus proposed updates, gaps and at most three immediate next actions. Say when there is nothing requiring action. Log routine checks; follow my notification preference for no-change runs. Surface access failures that prevent promised coverage.
```

### Task: Follow-up and Interview Reminder Review

Suggested setup: Optional daily or weekday review. Suggested window: overdue actions and the next seven days.

## 11 A simple daily and weekly routine

Each day: run or read Email Review if enabled; inspect new roles; choose one useful application, conversation or preparation action. You do not need to act on every recommendation.

Each week: use Weekly Review to look at real movement and capacity, select a few priorities, and check company/event discoveries as useful. Relationship audits help choose contacts; they do not require contacting every strong relationship.

Example: an email confirms a submitted application. Email Review matches the existing role, records supported evidence, preserves the application date and clears application-queue rank under the tracker rules. A repeat job alert is deduplicated. A later interview request creates a scheduling action. Interview Prep uses the same Role ID. A debrief adds reusable learning, and Weekly Review considers the next step.

Every record change depends on evidence and authorized access. In manual mode, the assistant proposes the exact edits and you make them.



## 12 Manual use, missing access and new chats

No email connector: paste relevant messages with employer/role, original date and thread context, including replies already sent. Redact unrelated or sensitive details. The assistant can draft a briefing and proposed edits, but cannot claim a complete inbox review.

No research access: supply postings or organizer pages. Ask for assessment of the supplied material and a list of facts you need to verify. Do not describe those sources as freshly checked.

No spreadsheet writes: ask for a change list with stable record ID, field, old/new value, evidence and unresolved questions. Apply it in your copy, then share the changed rows for verification. Do not count proposed updates as saved.

No scheduler: put reminders in a calendar or checklist yourself. At the chosen time, paste the Common task instructions and matching Task block into a chat with current inputs. The workflow works manually.

No cross-chat memory: share your Workspace Map and current context packet at each new chat. Keep facts and current rules in durable files, and ask the chat to confirm access. When a conversation grows unwieldy, save a short continuation note with current decisions, pending work and evidence references, then start a new chat with the same working prompt.

The private development handoffs are not required. Your prompts, current rule files, map, tracker and evidence are your operating system.



## 13 Keep the system reliable

After your first run, check one role update, one drafted-versus-sent message, one help offer versus completed referral, and one dated Dashboard result against the underlying records. Use a temporary working copy for fictional test data; keep real records and the distributed template clean.

For each chosen task, verify scheduled access and output delivery. A successful manual run does not prove its scheduled version works. If a test fails, record the limitation and use the manual path.

When changing rules, preserve document and task identity, record the change inside the document and update saved task instructions. Keep filenames descriptive and stable, without dates or versions. Never distribute your private profile, correspondence, contacts or file links in the blank kit.

These prompts are ready to use, but provider-specific capabilities and independent-candidate usability still require testing. No automated account actions have been installed by creating this guide.



## 14 Reference: tracker rules

This section is a reference, not another setup step. Your Workspace Map is the file directory you filled in during setup and a record of tool access and permissions. References such as Current Resume, Career Evidence, Search Strategy and Tracker Roles mean the locations you put in that map. Mark files you do not yet have Missing; create them only when useful.

The Tracker has eight tabs: Dashboard, Roles, People, Target Companies, Networking Events, Activities & Follow-ups, Interviews, and How This Works. Read How This Works for dropdown definitions and record limits. Detailed career stories, interview briefs, learning notes and task run logs belong in documents in your own folder, referenced from the map.

The blank template separates a role’s Hiring stage, Process outcome, Posting status and Fit decision. Priority is P1, P2 or Watch under its documented definitions; employment type is separate. Rank orders active pre-application Found roles marked Pursue. Applied date preserves confirmed submission history. Stage change date changes only when Hiring stage changes.

Planned actions and due dates belong in Activities & Follow-ups. A draft is not a sent message; a help offer is not a completed referral; a registration is not attendance. Stable IDs link related records across tabs. The Dashboard calculates recorded activity within its chosen date range; it does not discover activity.

If you already have another tracker, map its actual fields and rules first. Keep its schema, dropdowns and established definitions. Do not copy this template’s statuses into it or add removed fields without an agreed migration.
